# RailOptimus
**AI-powered decision support for real-time railway operations**

RailOptimus is a real-time, AI-assisted system that helps railway section controllers make **safe, efficient, and explainable dispatching decisions** under uncertainty. The system combines **optimization, machine learning, and a digital twin simulator** to proactively resolve conflicts and minimize delays.

Developed as part of **Smart India Hackathon (SIH) 2025**, but designed as a **production-oriented decision-support system**.

## Problem
Railway traffic control involves complex, time-critical decisions (routing, crossings, platform allocation) that are largely manual and reactive, leading to cascading delays and inefficient track utilization.

---

## Solution
- **Digital twin** of railway sections for simulation and safety validation  
- **Hybrid optimization engine** (CP-SAT / MILP + heuristics) for conflict-free routing  
- **Rolling-horizon re-optimization** triggered by delays or disruptions  
- **ML-based predictions** for delay, speed, and dwell times  
- **Explainable recommendations** with alternatives and KPI impact

---

## Impact
- Reduced secondary delays and travel time  
- Improved section throughput and block utilization  
- Faster recovery from disruptions  
- Safety guaranteed by design (headways, block occupancy, platform constraints)
